My Story

People infrastructure in Shanghai. Product toolkit at Michigan. Technical depth in e-commerce and data warehousing.

At OVO, I started closest to the business — a forward-deployed PM in the customer care domain, shipping data products under data mesh principles and proving they could drive real value. From there I moved into core platform APIs, then took full ownership of the data platform: governance, discovery, cost optimisation, platform operating model, observability.

Every move was a zoom out — from one domain's data products, to the APIs everyone depends on, to the platform that underpins it all. Now I'm sole PM on a team of three, covering 21 capabilities and 20+ consuming teams.

The thread through all of it: I build the trust and governance layers that let organisations make confident decisions.
