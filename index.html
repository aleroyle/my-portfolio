My Story

People infrastructure in Shanghai. Product toolkit at Michigan. Technical depth in e-commerce and data warehousing.

At OVO, I started closest to the business — a forward-deployed PM in the customer care domain, shipping data products and proving they could drive real value. From there I moved into core platform APIs, then took full ownership of the data platform: the self-serve infrastructure that manages the entire data product lifecycle.

Every move was a zoom out — from one domain's data products, to the APIs everyone depends on, to the platform that underpins it all. Now I own 23 capabilities across ingestion, transformation, publishing, governance, discovery, and consumption — applying data mesh principles pragmatically so 20+ domain teams can self-serve.

The thread through all of it: I build the self-serve infrastructure and governance layers that let domain teams ship trusted data products independently.

What I'm Known For

Self-Serve Data Platform

I build the end-to-end infrastructure — ingestion pipelines, managed transformation environments, data product publishing, and a marketplace for discovery and consumption. Domain teams self-serve without central team dependency. Data mesh principles applied pragmatically, not dogmatically.

Infrastructure-Enforced Governance

Column-level security, architectural constraints, and data quality observability — all baked into the platform. Compliance through code: auditable access grants via GitHub PRs, multi-region replication, taxonomy design, and automated quality monitoring. Not policy documents.

Evidence-Led Platform Strategy

Analytical frameworks, cost analysis, and behavioural analytics that shape where leadership invests. I build the evidence packs — redundancy classification, discovery funnels, effort-value models — and present them from SQL deep-dives to c-suite strategy decks.
